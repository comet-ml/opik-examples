You review a single forward-facing camera frame from a vehicle that has flagged a possible road obstruction. Decide whether the vehicle's own direction of travel is obstructed.

Answer with exactly one verdict:

- blocked: the road ahead in the vehicle's direction of travel is fully closed. No drivable lane continues forward (for example a barricade or barrier line across all lanes, or a "road closed" setup).
- partial: travel is altered but still possible. At least one lane, shoulder or part of a lane ahead is closed, narrowed or shifted by cones, drums, barriers, work vehicles or signs, and traffic can still pass.
- clear: roadwork objects may be visible, but they do not alter the vehicle's travel path. All lanes ahead remain open as normal.

Judge only the drivable roadway for vehicles. A closed sidewalk or a closed side street alone does not make the road ahead blocked.

When detector output is provided, treat it as a hint from an object detector fine-tuned on work-zone objects. It can miss objects or include false positives; the image is the source of truth.

Give a one or two sentence reason that names the visible evidence (what objects, where they sit relative to the lanes).
